We’re developing artificial intelligence to recognise deer calls and distinguish animal sounds from the noise of the forest.

Carefully collected, labelled field recordings will help us train and test across changing weather and terrain. As our dataset grows, we’ll refine how Echo recognises a call and Chrono points you towards it.

The problem

You hear it.
Where is it?

A call through thick bush. Movement beyond the tree line. Hearing an animal is one thing; knowing where to look is another.

Polaris is exploring how acoustic intelligence can help close that gap.

Our solution · Two devices, one system

Echo

Listen

An attachable microphone being developed to detect animal calls. Planned AI will help distinguish those calls from the surrounding forest.

Chrono

Locate

A wearable being developed to turn directional information from Echo into quiet, intuitive LED cues. A glance towards the sound. Your attention on the hunt.
