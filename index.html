Every industry has moments when a new idea changes what's possible. Hunting has benefited from better optics, navigation and equipment, yet understanding what's happening beyond your line of sight remains a challenge.

We founded Polaris to change that. We're developing an AI-powered acoustic tool that listens for deer calls and helps hunters make more informed decisions in the field. Not to replace instinct or experience, but to support it.

We're two founders who believe the next evolution of hunting technology should respect the traditions that came before it. And we're excited to bring that vision to life.

Two devices. One system. In development.

Echo

An attachable microphone being developed to recognise deer calls and locate their source.

Chrono

A wearable being developed to turn Echo’s directional information into quiet, intuitive LED cues.

A planned path for Echo and Chrono, shaped by what we learn from hunters along the way.

Polaris / Field notesIn development
Listen. Learn. Refine.Shaped by fieldwork.

  1. Research

    First, we listen.

    Gather carefully labelled deer calls and listen to hunters, so the work begins with real sounds and real needs.

  2. Field Testing

    Take it beyond the lab.

    Test how well we can recognise calls and their direction through wind, weather and changing terrain.

  3. Prototype Trials

    Put it in hunters’ hands.

    Trial Echo and Chrono with hunters of different experience levels, refining the fit, feedback and information that matters.

  4. Early Access

    Open the next chapter.

    Invite our early community to experience the tools when they’re ready, and keep improving them through time in the field.
