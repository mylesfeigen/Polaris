The roadmap

From sound to field — a clear path forward.

We’re building Polaris in stages, from real-world recordings to time in hunters’ hands. Each step is a chance to test an idea, learn something useful and refine what comes next.

  1. 01 Collect

    Build the dataset.

    Gather wildlife recordings, environmental context and carefully checked observations from the field.

  2. 02 Develop

    Train the technology.

    Train and evaluate AI models against real recordings to distinguish deer calls from forest noise.

  3. 03 Prototype

    Create the hardware.

    Develop Echo’s acoustic attachment and Chrono’s directional wearable around practical use outdoors.

  4. 04 Validate

    Test in real conditions.

    Evaluate detection, direction and reliability through changing weather and terrain with hunters.

  5. 05 Trial

    Invite early users.

    Invite our early community to try the tools when ready and help shape the experience in the field.
