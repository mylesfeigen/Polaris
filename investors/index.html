The opportunity

The forest has more
to tell us.

A hunter can hear a call without knowing where it came from. Dense cover, changing terrain and background noise make the picture harder to read.

Polaris is developing two connected devices to help turn those sounds into useful direction, while keeping the hunter’s attention in the field.

Echo

An attachable microphone concept for detecting and interpreting animal calls.

Chrono

A wearable concept that communicates direction through simple light cues.

Our focus

What we’re
working towards.

Carefully collected data
Gather and label field recordings to support planned AI training and evaluation across different conditions.
Practical hardware
Develop early Echo and Chrono prototypes around clear feedback, everyday handling and outdoor use.
Testing in the field
Evaluate the concepts with hunters to understand their usefulness, limitations and the improvements that matter.
